System Capabilities

Features of MediCare

Every single feature is natively backed by dedicated ASP.NET Core Web API endpoints and Entity Framework entities.

🔐
Feature 01

Role-Based JWT Authentication

Secure login and registration with BCrypt password hashing supporting Patient, Caregiver, and Admin authorization roles.

POST /api/AuthController/login
💊
Feature 02

Prescription & Inventory Management

Store full medicine catalogs with precise dosage quantities, start/end regimen dates, and stock tracking.

GET/POST /api/medicine
⏰
Feature 03

Multi-Time Routine Scheduler

Configure morning, afternoon, and night timing slots per medicine with automated conflict prevention.

GET/POST /api/medicineschedule
✅
Feature 04

One-Tap "Mark As Taken"

Real-time intake recording that logs the timestamp, marks status as Taken, and auto-decrements current pill stock.

PUT /api/medicinelog/{id}/taken
⏳
Feature 05

Dynamic Dose Snoozing

Allows patients to delay a scheduled dose by customizable 15-minute intervals while recording audit trail snooze counters.

PUT /api/medicinelog/{id}/snooze
🚨
Feature 06

Instant SOS Distress Broadcast

One-click emergency trigger that immediately generates high-priority alerts across both in-app banners and automated email dispatches.

POST /api/emergency
🚑
Feature 07

Emergency Incident Resolution

Lifecycle tracking for active emergency alerts allowing Caregivers or Admins to mark incidents as resolved with timestamped notes.

PUT /api/emergency/{id}/resolve
🔗
Feature 08

Caregiver-Patient Linking Engine

Connects family members and nursing staff to patient profiles using simple email lookups or secure relationship IDs.

POST /api/Caregiverpatient/link
👥
Feature 09

Contact Cards & Patient Switcher

Gives caregivers quick access to emergency phone numbers, date-of-birth, and current compliance scores for all linked individuals.

GET /api/caregiver/patients-contact-cards
⚠️
Feature 10

Critical Low-Stock Warning Engine

Monitors pill inventories across all prescriptions and generates proactive refill warnings whenever stock falls below threshold levels (≤5).

GET /api/medicine/low-stock
📊
Feature 11

Periodic Adherence Rate Calculations

Computes exact compliance percentages across Daily, Weekly, or Monthly periods to evaluate patient health progress.

GET /api/adherence/patient/{id}/report
🤖
Feature 12

AI Medical Assistant

Role-aware AI chatbot interface providing immediate answers regarding prescription timings, meal considerations, and safe usage guidelines.

POST /api/chat/ask
🖨️
Feature 13

Printable Clinical Health Dossier

Direct HTML/CSS doctor report generator equipped with pre-formatted printable CSS ready for A4 paper and PDF downloads.

GET /api/report/patient/{id}/print
🔔
Feature 14

In-App Notification Dispatcher

Stores and retrieves system notifications, dose reminders, and caregiver link confirmations with single or batch read markers.

GET/PUT /api/notification
💼
Feature 15

Enterprise Admin Control Center

System-wide operational dashboard summarizing total users, patient counts, system-wide adherence averages, and active emergencies.

GET /api/dashboard/admin
